Letter Racers

A 3D driving game that helps young children learn the English alphabet.

Privacy Policy

Letter Racers does not collect any personal information. It needs no account or login, shows no ads, contains no tracking or analytics, and does not connect to the internet. Lesson progress is stored only on the device and is removed when the app is deleted.
